# Code of conduct

We want GymFlow to be a project where anyone can take part without being made to feel
unwelcome.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient. People here have different backgrounds, first languages and levels
  of experience.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully when you get something wrong.
- Keep discussion on the project.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere else someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these rules, email **support@gymflow.sbs**.
Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block
someone from the project where that is needed.
