How to set up GymFlow for your gym

  1. Create your gym account. Sign up on the web; no card needed for the 14-day free trial.
  2. Import your member records. Upload your Excel, CSV or XLS member list; GymFlow normalises phone numbers and matches your plans.
  3. Configure memberships and payments. Set up plans, prices and payment details in the app.
  4. Start managing your gym. Track members, attendance, payments, reports and WhatsApp reminders from one place.

Gym software FAQ: everything you need to know

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 14 days with every feature unlocked and no credit card required. Import your members, send WhatsApp reminders, and run the 14-day free trial before you decide.

How much does GymFlow cost?

Start free: the free forever plan covers up to 50 members. Paid plans are ₹999 per month or ₹9,999 for a year (save 17%), with every feature, unlimited members, and unlimited WhatsApp messages. Gyms on the free plan can add WhatsApp reminders for ₹299 per month. No GST, no per-member charges, and no setup fee.

Are WhatsApp messages really unlimited?

Yes, on the paid plans. Welcome messages, renewal reminders, and payment due alerts are all included, so there is no per-message charge. On the free plan, WhatsApp reminders are a ₹299 per month add-on.

Can I move my existing members from Excel?

Upload your CSV or Excel file and GymFlow matches your column headings to its own fields, then shows a preview so you can correct anything before saving. Nothing is written until you confirm.

Is my gym's data safe?

Each gym's data is kept separate, and you sign in with your own account. Data is encrypted in transit. You can export members, attendance and payments to Excel, and delete your gym's data from account settings.

How fast does support reply?

Every paid plan includes direct founder support. Message us on WhatsApp or email support@gymflow.sbs and we reply within 24 hours.

How do I reach support?

Over WhatsApp, phone, or email. You talk to the person who builds GymFlow rather than a ticket queue, and we reply within 24 hours. Support in English and Tamil.

Where can I check if GymFlow is working?

Our public status page at status.gymflow.sbs shows whether GymFlow is running normally and lists any issues we are working on. If it shows all clear and something still looks wrong, message us on WhatsApp or email support@gymflow.sbs.

Your gym's data stays in your control

Each gym's data is kept separate and is reached through that gym's own account. The site and app are served over HTTPS.
