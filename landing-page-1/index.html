How to set up GymFlow for your gym

  1. Sign up and onboard. A six-step wizard walks you through your gym name, membership plans, pricing and WhatsApp details. It autosaves, so you can stop and come back.
  2. Import your members. Upload the CSV or Excel sheet you already keep. GymFlow matches your column headings to its own fields and shows you a preview, so you can fix anything odd before a single record is saved.
  3. Run the day to day. Mark attendance, record payments, and send WhatsApp reminders for dues — from one dashboard that works just as well on your phone.
  4. Analyse and grow. Monthly revenue charts, plan distribution, joining and expiry trends, and PDF reports you can download. Know exactly where the gym stands before the month closes.

₹3,000 per month, flat. One plan with every feature, unlimited members, and unlimited WhatsApp messages. No per-member charges, no setup fee, and no modules to unlock later.

Are WhatsApp messages really unlimited?

Yes. Welcome messages, renewal reminders, and payment due alerts are all included. Most gym platforms bill ₹0.30 to ₹1 per message, which becomes thousands a month once you pass 200 members.

Can I move my existing members from Excel?

Upload your CSV or Excel file and GymFlow matches your column headings to its own fields, then shows a preview so you can correct anything before saving. Nothing is written until you confirm, and most gyms finish a few hundred members in under 15 minutes.

Is my gym's data safe?

Each gym's records are isolated and reachable only from your own account. Data is encrypted in transit, backed up regularly, and never shared with other gyms or sold on.
