1. Introduction

GymFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our gym management platform at www.gymflow.sbs and the application at app.gymflow.sbs.

GymFlow is built for independent gym owners in India. When you sign up as a gym owner, you are our customer. The members you add to your gym are your customers — for their data, you are the data controller and GymFlow acts as your processor. This distinction runs throughout this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • Gym business information — gym name, address, membership plans and pricing.
  • Member data — the member records you enter into the platform, such as names, contact numbers, membership plans, attendance and payment history.
  • UPI / payment details — to help you collect payments, we store your gym's UPI merchant details (such as your UPI ID and payee name) so the platform can generate a UPI QR code. We do not store card numbers, bank credentials, or the raw QR image, and we do not process or hold funds — payments settle directly to your own UPI account.
  • Usage data and analytics — how you interact with the platform, device and browser information, and diagnostic logs used to keep the service reliable.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • Verify your subscription payments and send related information.
  • Generate UPI QR codes from your gym's UPI details so you can collect payments from members.
  • Send WhatsApp notifications to gym members.
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and support requests.
  • Send transactional emails, such as account verification and security notifications.
  • Monitor and analyse trends, usage, and activity to improve the platform.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, and abuse.

4. WhatsApp Messaging

WhatsApp notifications are a core part of GymFlow and are active for every gym. We use the WhatsApp Business API (provided by Meta) to send transactional messages to gym members on behalf of their gym. These messages include welcome notifications, payment confirmations, and membership reminders.

Because these messages are sent using the member contact details a gym enters, gym owners are responsible for obtaining consent from their members to be contacted on WhatsApp, and for ensuring the messages sent through their account comply with WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy and applicable law. Members can ask their gym to stop messaging them at any time.

5. Member Data & Your Role as a Gym Owner

Member data belongs to the gym that entered it. As a gym owner, you decide what member information to collect and how it is used within your gym. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to collect and store that information, and for honouring your members' requests to access, correct, or delete their data.

GymFlow processes member data only to provide the service to you, and never uses one gym's member data to benefit another gym. If you close your account, we handle member data as described in Data Retention below.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers (sub-processors) that help us run the platform, and only as needed to provide the service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage (for example, gym logos).
  • Vercel — application hosting and content delivery.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Cloud API) — sending WhatsApp messages to gym members.
  • Upstash — QStash to queue and rate-limit outgoing WhatsApp messages, and Redis for rate limiting.
  • Sentry — error and performance monitoring, and session replay, to diagnose problems (see Analytics & Error Tracking).
  • Resend — sending transactional email, such as account verification and security notifications.
  • Groq — generating draft replies to emails sent to our support inbox. The sender's name, the gym name on their account and the text of their message are sent to it so our team can review and edit a draft; nothing is sent automatically, and member records are not sent.
  • Google Maps Platform — address autocomplete when you enter your gym's address during onboarding.

These providers process data only on our instructions and under their own security and privacy obligations.

We may also disclose information:

  • To legal authorities — when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.
  • In a business transfer — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

7. Analytics & Error Tracking

To keep the platform reliable, we use Sentry to capture errors, performance data, and session replays (recordings of how a page looked and behaved, used to reproduce a problem). In the owner console and the member app, a replay captures what is shown on screen and what is tapped or typed, which can include member names, phone numbers, membership and payment details and other information entered into the app. Password and one-time-code fields are masked and are never recorded. A replay is always kept when an error occurs, and a small share of ordinary sessions is also recorded.

IP addresses, cookies and request bodies are stripped from error reports before they are sent. Replays and error reports are used only by GymFlow staff to find and fix faults, are held by Sentry for a limited period under its retention settings, and are not used for advertising or sold. If you do not want your sessions recorded, contact us and we will arrange for them to be excluded where we reasonably can.

On our public marketing site (www.gymflow.sbs) we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the page, through heatmaps and anonymised session recordings. Clarity may set a first-party cookie. It masks text typed into form fields, and we do not send it names, phone numbers or other account details. Clarity is not used inside the owner console or the member app. See Microsoft's privacy statement.

We also use Google Analytics on the public marketing site to measure visits, traffic sources and which sections of the page are used. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects device, browser and approximate location information, in aggregate, and does not receive names, phone numbers or other account details. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

We do not use third-party advertising or sell your data. We monitor overall search performance for our public marketing site using tools such as Google Search Console, which rely on aggregated crawl and search data and do not track individual visitors of the application.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data. This includes encryption of data in transit, secure authentication and row-level access controls. Our database provider maintains its own backups under its terms; we do not currently offer customer-initiated restore. Each gym's data is isolated so that it is only accessible to authorised users of that gym.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so while we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. When you delete your gym account, the associated gym and member data are removed: the owner deletes the gym, its members and the associated login, confirmed by a one-time code. Our database provider maintains its own backups under its terms; we do not currently offer customer-initiated restore. We may keep records we are legally required to keep. You can also request deletion of your data by contacting us.

10. Exporting and Deleting Your Data

Owners can export members, attendance and payments to Excel and reports to PDF. Owners can delete all members, or the whole gym, from account settings. Members can ask their gym, or write to support@gymflow.sbs.

11. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You may also object to certain processing or request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@gymflow.sbs. If you are a gym member, please contact your gym first, as they control your records; we will support your gym in fulfilling your request.

12. Children's Privacy

GymFlow is intended for use by gym owners and their operations. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a gym adds a member who is a minor, the gym is responsible for obtaining any consent required from a parent or guardian.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the platform or by email. Your continued use of GymFlow after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
