2. Description of Service

GymFlow is a gym management platform that lets gym owners manage members, track payments and dues, record attendance, create membership plans, import members from Excel or CSV files, run reports, and send WhatsApp messages to members. Paid plans also include the member app, where members see their progress, streaks, points, badges and leaderboards, and where owners can award prizes and run challenges. The Service is delivered over the web at app.gymflow.sbs.

GymFlow is software only. We do not process, hold or settle any money between a gym and its members: UPI QR codes and links created in the app send payments straight to the gym's own UPI account, and the owner records them in GymFlow.

4. Plans, Subscription and Payments

GymFlow is offered on the following terms:

  • Free trial — new gyms get a 14-day free trial with every feature unlocked and no payment required. During the trial, importing from a file can bring in at most 50 members in total.
  • Free plan — when the trial ends you can choose to move to the Free plan instead of paying. It is limited to 50 members and does not include the member app. WhatsApp messages on the Free plan need the paid add-on below. The Free plan is available only after the trial and only if the gym has 50 members or fewer.
  • Paid plans — monthly and yearly plans with unlimited members, WhatsApp messaging and the member app, at the prices shown on our pricing page. Prices are in Indian Rupees (INR). A paid plan is never moved to the Free plan automatically.
  • WhatsApp add-on (Free plan only) — Rs 299 for 30 days of WhatsApp messaging, activated once we approve the payment. Paying early adds 30 days to the end of the current period. It is not needed on paid plans.
  • Payment by UPI — subscription payments are made by UPI to the details shown in the app, and you submit the payment reference. We check each payment by hand before activating your plan or add-on. We do not store card details or keep a payment method on file.
  • No auto-renewal — plans and add-ons do not renew automatically. You renew by making a new payment. We may remind you before a plan ends.
  • Refunds — fees already paid for a billing period are non-refundable, except where the law requires otherwise.
  • When a plan ends — when the trial or a paid plan ends, you are taken to the subscription page until you renew or, if you are eligible, move to the Free plan. We do not delete your data because a plan ended.
